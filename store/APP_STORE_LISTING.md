## Description
PintSize AI is a private artificial-intelligence assistant that runs entirely on your iPhone. Every conversation, voice reply, and generated image is produced on-device — nothing is sent to the cloud, and no account is required.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Chat with powerful open language models, fully offline
• Talk hands-free in Voice Mode — it listens, thinks, and speaks back
• Speak replies in a natural voice, or even your own with Apple Personal Voice
• Generate images from text on-device with Stable Diffusion
• Ask about documents (PDF, text, Word) — it reads and answers
• Get live stock prices with charts, and pull in web results when you want them
• Ask Siri questions and get answers from your on-device model

CHOOSE YOUR MODEL
• Browse and download the latest models live from Hugging Face
• Clear "fits your device" guidance so you pick one that runs well
• Manage storage — see sizes and delete models anytime

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All AI runs locally on your device
• No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking
• Your chats are stored only on your phone

PintSize AI puts a genuinely private AI in your pocket — pint-size, but capable.
